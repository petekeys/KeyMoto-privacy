KeyMoto Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

KeyMoto is a motorcycle route-planning and navigation application.

This privacy policy explains what information KeyMoto accesses and how it is used.

Location

KeyMoto uses your device location while the app is in use in order to:

  • show your current position on the map;
  • use your current position as the start of a route;
  • calculate routes;
  • provide navigation and route-progress information.

KeyMoto does not use background location.

Your location is processed on your device for routing and navigation. KeyMoto does not intentionally send your location to the developer.

Location information may be supplied to third-party services where necessary to provide features described below.

Place search

KeyMoto uses Geoapify to provide place, address and point-of-interest search suggestions.

When you use place search, the search text you enter and location information used to bias or restrict search results may be sent to Geoapify.

Online maps

KeyMoto uses online map services to display its basemap.

Requests to map providers may include normal internet connection information such as your IP address and the map area being requested.

KeyMoto currently uses MapLibre for map display and OpenFreeMap for online map content.

Information stored on your device

KeyMoto may store information locally on your device, including:

  • application settings;
  • enjoyment and routing preferences;
  • recently selected search places;
  • downloaded or packaged routing-map data.

This information is stored within the application's private storage.

Local application data can be removed by clearing KeyMoto's application data or uninstalling the application.

Accounts, advertising and sale of data

KeyMoto does not require you to create an account.

KeyMoto does not sell personal information.

KeyMoto does not use your personal information for advertising.

Data sharing

KeyMoto only shares information with third-party services where required to provide application functionality, such as place search and online map display.

Data security

KeyMoto uses Android's application storage protections for locally stored application data and secure network connections when communicating with supported online services.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored by KeyMoto on your device remains there until it is replaced, cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to those providers' own retention policies.

KeyMoto does not maintain a user account or a separate developer-held database of individual users' location histories.
